Privacy Policy

Last Updated: May 25, 2026

Welcome to My Warehouse ("the Application"). We are committed to protecting your personal information and your right to privacy. If you have any questions or concerns about this privacy policy, please contact us.

1. Information We Collect

We collect personal information that you voluntarily provide to us when registering or logging in on the Application. This includes:

  • Phone Number: Used strictly as your unique identification and account login credential to safely retrieve your ledger and warehouse inventories.
  • Inventory Records: Cold storage item details, weights, booking requests, and transactions linked to your profile to provide our core warehousing services.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect solely for business purposes to deliver services, specifically to:

  • Authenticate user logins and manage sessions.
  • Provide real-time inventory listings, stock receipts (MRs), and ledger details.
  • Generate demand and booking requests.
  • Notify you regarding app updates or transaction confirmations.

3. Data Security & Retention

We implement comprehensive technical and organizational security measures to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, alteration, or disclosure. We retain your information for as long as necessary to fulfill the services outline in this policy, unless a longer retention period is required by law.

4. Account & Data Deletion (Your Rights)

We respect your privacy and provide you complete control over your credentials and registered accounts. You can request your account and associated mobile number deletion at any time.

How to Delete Your Account:

You can permanently delete your credentials and remove your registered phone number directly inside the app. Open the Sidebar Menu Drawer → Tap on Settings → Scroll to the bottom and click on Delete Account (खाता हटाएं). This action is permanent, securely removes your login data, and wipes all linked credentials.

5. Updates to This Policy

We may update this privacy policy from time to time. The updated version will be indicated by an updated "Last Updated" date and the updated version will be effective as soon as it is accessible.

6. Contact Us

If you have any questions or suggestions about this Privacy Policy, do not hesitate to contact us at warehousingindi@gmail.com.
